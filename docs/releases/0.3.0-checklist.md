# 0.3.0 review checklist

Preparation is not publication. Do not push a tag, merge to `main`, or run a
publishing workflow until those actions are explicitly approved.

## Review scope

Review these files explicitly; do not stage the entire research checkout:

- `Cargo.toml`, `Cargo.lock`, `README.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `ROADMAP.md`.
- `docs/releasing.md`, `docs/releases/0.3.0.md`, this checklist, and
  `docs/releases/0.3.0-structural-benchmark.json` and the checkpoint benchmark.
- `.github/workflows/ci.yml` (synthetic adaptive example smoke test).
- `.github/workflows/audit.yml` (read-only direct cargo-audit invocation).
- `docs/worldquant-brain.md` and `examples/worldquant-brain/` (synthetic offline
  guide files and smoke test; no platform adapter or private data).
- `crates/alphawinnow/src/{artifact,feedback,lib,main,measured_search,transform,tree}.rs`.
- `crates/alphawinnow/src/{field_diversity,identity_reduction}.rs`.
- `crates/alphawinnow/src/parquet_input.rs` (ZSTD/uncompressed fixture parity).
- `crates/alphawinnow/examples/{adaptive_measured_search,measured_search_stdio,checkpoint_history}.rs`.
- `crates/alphawinnow/tests/{cli,measured_search,measured_bootstrap}.rs`.

- `AGENTS.md`: remove the private machine-specific research handoff; private
  instructions stay outside the repository. The unused `.dockerignore` was removed.

Keep private research folders and historical unpublished `0.2.1` preparation
files outside this release commit. Preserve them locally. The old benchmark is not validation
of the current source. Homebrew/Scoop are updated only by the publishing workflow
after new archives exist.

  to preserve the prepared `0.3.0` version.

Actual local results are recorded in [the release notes](0.3.0.md). Checklist
items are review gates, not a claim that external CI or publication occurred.
